# Stark Sky

A 3D city you fly through. Every tower is one of my projects. Fly the armored hero into a glowing ring to open it.

**Play:** https://kanishksaini-007.github.io/stark-sky/

I made this to learn 3D on the web. It is built with [three.js](https://threejs.org/) (WebGL) on a plain page, no game engine and no framework.

## What is in it
- A procedural high-tech city: towers, neon, flying traffic, moon and stars
- 12 landmark towers, one for each of my projects, plus About and Contact rings
- A red-and-gold armored hero built from basic 3D shapes, with a walk and flight pose
- Collision with buildings, roof landing, a chase camera, 60 energy cores to collect
- Synthesized sound (Web Audio), a radar, and a project list if you do not want to play
- Desktop (keyboard + mouse) and mobile (touch stick + buttons)

## Controls
`W A S D` move, `Space` up, `C` down, `Shift` boost, `E` enter, drag to look. On mobile: left stick, drag the right side, buttons for up, down, boost.

## Honest limits
The hero and buildings are simple shapes, not hand-modelled art. Phones with weak GPUs may run it at a lower resolution. The city is generated, so it is not a real map.

Learning project.
